🔗 **Website & Download: [www.valorant-spind.de](https://www.valorant-spind.de)**

Valorant-Spind is a free Windows tool that reads your Valorant locker locally and shows the total value of your skins in Euros – automatically, without looking up a single skin by hand. On top of that you get a personal coach based on your real matches, a kill heatmap and 40+ stats.

![Valorant skin value and collection](https://www.valorant-spind.de/valorant-spind-sammlung-wert.jpg)

## ✨ Features
- 💰 **Skin value in Euros** – your Valorant inventory value, calculated automatically from your locker
- 🎯 **Personal coach** – analysis of your real ranked matches: what to train, where you lose rounds
- 🗺️ **Kill heatmap** – see where you get kills and where you die on the real map
- 📊 **40+ stats & breakdowns** – form detection, trends, best playtime and more

![Personal coach from your real matches](https://www.valorant-spind.de/valorant-spind-coach.jpg)

## 🔒 Security & Privacy
- Runs **100% locally** on your PC
- **No login, no account, no password prompt**
- **No data transfer** to any server or third party
- Uses only locally available data from the Valorant client (read-only)
